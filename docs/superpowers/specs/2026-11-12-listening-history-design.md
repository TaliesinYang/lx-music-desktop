# Local listening history

Record a playback once when actual listening time reaches the smaller of 30 seconds and half of a known song duration; when duration is unknown use 30 seconds. Pauses, buffering and seek jumps do not add listening time. Replaying the same song starts a new session. Store track ID, source, title, artist, album, duration and timestamp only on this device. Recording is enabled by default at the user's request and can be disabled or cleared from playback settings. Do not collect lyrics, URLs, or audio data. Expose recent events and aggregate track counts via the existing protected loopback API. Recommendations can be based on those counts; genre inference remains separate and is not automatically uploaded. Existing prior listens cannot be reconstructed.
